# Costume Rental — Scope, version 3

## Booking
Customers book a costume through the website. A booking holds the item for 48 hours
before it is released back to stock. Each booking records one customer, one costume
and a hire window.

## Deposit
A deposit of 30% of the hire price is taken at the moment of booking. The remaining
balance is due on collection. Deposits are held against the booking, not against the
customer account.

## Cancellation
Customers may cancel up to 14 days before the hire date and receive a full refund of
the deposit. Cancellations inside 14 days forfeit the deposit.

## Support
The support team handles all cancellations and refunds by email. Any refund above
200 dollars must be approved by the owner before it is issued.
